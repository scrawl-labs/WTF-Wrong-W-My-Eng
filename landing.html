How it works

Talk. Get caught. Get better.

Three steps from awkward English to confident fluency.

01

Start Recording

Hit record and speak naturally. A job interview, a call, a presentation — Scoldler runs quietly in the background and captures everything you say.

02

Instant Analysis

Every sentence is transcribed on-device and checked for grammar errors, unnatural phrasing, and vocabulary. Results appear within seconds.

03

Review & Improve

Every session is saved. Review your corrections, see which mistakes repeat, and watch your English measurably improve over time.

Features

Everything in one window.

Real-time

Speech Transcription

Whisper-powered, on-device. Every word you say is captured with high accuracy, no matter the topic or background noise.

Grammar

Correction Engine

Wrong tense, wrong preposition, extra "to" — Scoldler finds it immediately and shows the corrected form alongside your original.

Expressions

Native Phrasing

Grammatically correct but still sounds off? Scoldler suggests how a native speaker would naturally say the same thing.

Vocabulary

Coaching in Context

Idioms and power phrases explained in context — not from a dictionary. You learn the words that actually come up in real conversation.

History

Session Archive

Every session is saved locally with full transcripts and correction breakdowns. Rename, review, and track your progress over time.

Local

Zero Cloud Dependency

Everything runs on-device via Ollama. No subscriptions, no data collection, no internet required after the initial model download.

Privacy

Your voice never leaves your Mac.

Every bit of processing happens locally. Your audio, your transcripts, your mistakes — all on-device. Scoldler uses Ollama to run models entirely on your machine.
